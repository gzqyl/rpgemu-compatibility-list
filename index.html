💡 Pro-Tips for Playing Games on RPGEmu

  • MV Js error: If you find the screen "undefined...JS error", just restart the game or even restart the app, the issue will be solved.
  • MV save lost / crash: If you find after you saved the game from game side, and exit the game, when restart the game, can not continue, please switch to low resolution 2x, to save memory.
  • XP/VX/VXAce: Keep patching unless game crashed, we have bundled 10 patches for ruby games, almost 80% ruby games are supported to play indeed.
